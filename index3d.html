How to read the map

A line means one subreddit is among the other's 5 closest neighbours at that snapshot, measured by how alike their vocabulary is (cosine similarity of TF-IDF over their latest 100 posts). The word on the line is the single term that contributes most to that similarity.

Line length is the distance. Each line is a spring whose resting length is 40 + 280 × (1 − similarity / max) px: the more words two communities share, the shorter the spring pulls them. Springs compete, so a line can be stretched by everything else tugging on its two ends; the tooltip shows the resting length it wants.

No line between two bubbles means neither is in the other's top 5 at that snapshot: they may still share words, just fewer than their closer neighbours.

Bubble size = subscribers. Colour = theme. Only ~10 seed subreddits per theme were named by hand; the other 400 got their colour by label propagation, i.e. by copying whatever their closest neighbours are. Hover a theme chip for the three words its members lean on most.
